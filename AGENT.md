# Research and publication policy

Resolve every supplied organisation number against an official registry record or a declared public registry snapshot. Preserve one terminal result per input, including invalid inputs and acquisition failures.

Discovery candidates are hypotheses. Fetch the underlying permitted source, establish the exact entity and preserve raw evidence before publication. Registry relationships, websites for shared offices and similar names must not collapse parents, subsidiaries, holding companies or separate legal entities.

Company accounts use separate-entity filings and explicit reporting years. Missing values remain missing. Roles and registered workplaces retain their official relationship and availability semantics.

Jobs from NAV require fresh exact parent/subunit employer identifiers, active status and valid dates. A role card on a verified careers page may be described as a listed role; it does not prove individual-ad status. Company publications need a local title, publication date, source and company subject. Advice, section headings and news-index pages do not become company events.

Record source URL, retrieval timestamp, response-body hash, parser version and a verbatim source span. Capture corruption is diagnosed and quarantined, never repaired by relabelling bytes. Search and cached candidates do not supply publication evidence.

Respect public-address, robots, TLS and access policies at every redirect. No platform login, prohibited scraping, embedded personal keys or participant-hosted discovery service is required. Runtime never sends messages, publishes a website or submits an entry.

Refresh preserves prior evidence and reports changes with support for both versions. Failed acquisition retains prior supported facts as stale; missing list entries do not prove closure or resignation.
